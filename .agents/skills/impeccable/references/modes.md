# 🧭 Surface Modes — Determining Design Altitude

The surface mode dictates what success looks like for the visitor. Ambition scales with the mode:

---

## 1. Persuade Mode (Marketing & Acquisition)
- **Surfaces**: Landing pages, marketing websites, campaign microsites, product feature launches, pricing pages.
- **Visitor Success**: The visitor understands the value proposition, feels trust and excitement, and takes decisive action.
- **Design Philosophy**: Full commitment. Bold, distinct visual character with an undeniable point of view. Memorable display typography, custom layout composition, high-contrast visual anchors, and dynamic pacing. Category habits and safe defaults are discarded in favor of brand differentiation.

---

## 2. Operate Mode (Application & Productivity)
- **Surfaces**: Web applications, data dashboards, developer consoles, admin portals, editors, settings panels, transactional flows.
- **Visitor Success**: The visitor executes high-frequency tasks with speed, precision, zero cognitive friction, and muscle memory.
- **Design Philosophy**: Restraint and precision. Visual scanability, consistent spatial grids, predictable keyboard navigation, and fast render times outrank novelty. Brand identity lives in the micro-details: crisp hairlines, subtle active states, beautiful focus rings, and readable tabular numbers.

---

## 3. Read Mode (Knowledge & Long-Form)
- **Surfaces**: Documentation, knowledge bases, technical specifications, guides, engineering blogs, changelogs.
- **Visitor Success**: The visitor absorbs complex conceptual material without cognitive fatigue or visual distraction.
- **Design Philosophy**: Typographic cadence and reading ergonomics. Optimal line measure (65–75 characters), comfortable line-height (1.6–1.75), muted secondary notes, clear heading hierarchy, responsive code blocks, and distraction-free layout.

---

## 4. Experience Mode (Immersive Showcases)
- **Surfaces**: Creative portfolios, agency showcases, interactive exhibits, product galleries, digital installations.
- **Visitor Success**: The visitor is immersed inside the work itself; the creative artifact commands attention.
- **Design Philosophy**: The interface recedes. Chrome is minimal, floating, or contextual. The canvas, media, typography, or 3D viewport leads from the first pixel. Subtle motion, depth, and spatial audio/haptics amplify the sensory experience.
