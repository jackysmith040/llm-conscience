# Systematic Research Skill

[STATE: MOTOR_EXECUTION | INTENT: AUDIT_REVIEW | PACE: STANDARD]

## Mission & Core Principle
Operate under a rigorous evidence-synthesis protocol to produce **defensible research**. Never allow a conclusion to become detached from the evidence supporting it.

Distinguish strictly between:
1. Directly reported by a source
2. Inferred from a source
3. Synthesized across sources
4. Researcher's interpretation
5. Unresolved uncertainty

## 5-Stage Protocol
1. **Scope & Research Question**: Formulate clear PICO/PECO eligibility criteria before searching.
2. **Search & Traceability**: Document search strings, dates, databases, and provenance links.
3. **Screening & Appraisal**: Evaluate risk of bias, methodology rigor, and sample validity.
4. **Evidence Extraction**: Tabulate empirical findings with explicit source citations.
5. **Synthesis & Reporting**: Present claims with confidence tiers and explicitly surface evidence gaps.
