# Diátaxis learning contract

Use Diátaxis to separate learner needs rather than mixing every purpose into one chapter. The four official modes are tutorial, how-to, reference, and explanation. Exercises form a connected practice layer.

## Tutorial

Guide the learner through a small successful learning path. State the goal, prerequisites, actions, expected observations, motion sequence, pauses, and checkpoint. Prefer concrete progress over exhaustive coverage.

## How-to

Help the learner accomplish a specific task. Give concise steps, prerequisites, decision points, failure recovery, and a completion check. Avoid turning the guide into a general theory essay.

## Reference

Provide exact, reliable lookup information: definitions, commands, formulas, parameters, conventions, signatures, constraints, and source locations. Organize for scanning. Do not hide crucial facts in narrative.

## Explanation

Explain why something exists, behaves, or is designed in a particular way. Include context, connections, assumptions, trade-offs, limits, and carefully bounded analogies.

## Exercises

Link exercises to the target understanding layer. Include reconstruction, prediction, application, variation, transfer, and generative tasks. Do not treat completion of a tutorial as mastery.

## Chapter contract

When the learner requests a chapter, use these labels:

1. Tutorial
2. How-to
3. Further information: Reference
4. Further information: Explanation
5. Exercises

For large chapters, divide each part into stable subsections with identifiers and cross-links. Keep the purpose of each section explicit: learn, accomplish, look up, understand why, or practice.
